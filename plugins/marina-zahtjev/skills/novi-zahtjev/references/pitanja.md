# Question bank — what to ask, how, and what never to ask

Formal Croatian ("Vi"). One question per message. Short. No preamble.

## The eight slots

### 1. Situacija — the last time it hurt
- "Opišite mi zadnji put kad Vam je ovo zasmetalo. Što se točno dogodilo?"
- "Koliko često se to događa — svaki dan, svaki tjedan, u sezoni?"
- If the opening message already tells a story, skip and reflect: "Dakle, prošli tjedan … Jesam li dobro razumio?"

### 2. Danas — how it is done now
- "Kako to danas rješavate? Tko to radi (koja uloga) i koliko traje?"
- "Gdje ti podaci danas stoje — u blagajni, u web shopu, u tablici, na papiru?"
- Record as fact. Never comment on it.

### 3. Drugačije — the outcome
- "Kad ovo bude gotovo, što će biti drugačije u Vašem danu?"
- "Koja je jedna stvar koja mora raditi da biste rekli: to je to?"
- Push once for something checkable: "Kako bismo to provjerili — što bi netko trebao vidjeti ili dobiti?"

### 4. Primjeri — real cases (Helm looks them up)
- "Dajte mi tri do pet stvarnih primjera: šifre artikala, brojevi narudžbi, datoteke ili poveznice."
- For each: look it up, then confirm — "Šifra 92250 je Sidro Bruce 10 kg, na zalihi 6, u dolasku 10. To je taj?"
- If they cannot give examples, that itself goes into "Otvoreno za Newton".

### 5. Vaše odluke — decisions only they own
Detect from the story; ask directly, one at a time. Typical decisions:
- limits and thresholds ("od kojeg iznosa", "koliko komada", "koliko dana")
- what happens on an exception ("ako artikl nema šifru u blagajni — preskočiti, zaustaviti ili javiti?")
- who pays a third-party cost (SMS, e-mail, servis dobavljača)
- how long data is kept
- which customers / which shop / which language it applies to
Phrase as their decision: "To je Vaša odluka — kako želite da se ponaša kad …?"
A question the colleague cannot own — one for the legal service, the group, or a
supplier — is not asked twice. Write it into section 10 under the right addressee
("Za pravnu službu ili grupu", "Za dobavljača / vanjski sustav").

### 6. Što ne treba — the client's own scope cut
For every optional-looking part the colleague mentioned:
- "Ako … ne bi bilo uključeno, bi li Vam promjena i dalje bila vrijedna?"
- "Što od ovoga želite i dalje raditi sami, bez promjene?"
Everything they let go of goes into "Što nam ne treba". This section keeps the request small.

### 7. Vanjske strane i rokovi
- "Ovisi li ovo o nekom vanjskom sustavu ili dobavljaču? Imate li od njih dokument, primjer datoteke ili kontakt (uloga)?"
- "Postoji li zakonski ili grupni rok? Što se dogodi ako se propusti?" — a fact, not a wish. Never ask when they would like it delivered.
- "Ima li razdoblje kad promjena na shopu ne dolazi u obzir (sezona, akcija, inventura)?"
- Ask for the artefact, not a description: "Možete li priložiti tu datoteku / taj dopis?" If the attached document carries other people's contact details, keep only the supplier's role and the document's name in the request.

### 8. Što Marina Stores priprema — the work order
Never ask "tko će kod vas …". Turn it into a sentence in the first person plural and
read it back: "Pripremit ćemo popis od dvadeset stvarnih pretraga koje kupci upisuju."
— "Stoji li tako?" Every confirmed sentence goes into section 8 of the request.

## Never ask
- who the administrator / process owner is
- whether they want two people trained
- whether they accept an assumption
- whether they confirm a boundary to another project
- whether the Lalizas group is interested
- when they want it delivered, order or date of go-live, choice of technical approach, notification e-mail address
- anything Helm can look up: does product X exist, what is its price, how many orders last week, is module Y installed, what does the error log say

## Never say
- a price, an hour count, a duration, a rate, a discount
- "jednostavno", "samo", "brzo" about the work — you do not know
- anything that judges how Marina Stores works today
- a promise about a third party (POS vendor, SAP, Cloudinary, payment provider)
- a technical word in a colleague-facing sentence: hook, cron, modul, API, endpoint, JSON, SQL, deploy, cache — say "veza", "automatska provjera", "dio shopa", "podaci"
